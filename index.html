Enyuan Zhao is a researcher at the Hangzhou Institute for Advanced Study, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). His research lies at the intersection of AI for Science (AI4S) and multimodal learning, with a focus on remote sensing vision-and-language understanding — including remote sensing visual question answering, image–text retrieval, and copy-move forgery understanding — as well as large language models for scientific prediction. His goal is to build general, domain-adaptable multimodal models and benchmarks that connect visual perception with scientific reasoning.

Research

  • AI for Science (AI4S)
  • Multimodal and vision-language learning
  • Remote sensing image understanding
  • Visual question answering and image–text retrieval
  • Large language models for scientific prediction
